能做什么What it does

从载荷到每个钢球From the loads to every ball

径向力、轴向力和力矩一起作用时，按内外圈的相对位移解出每个钢球的载荷和工作接触角；深沟球轴承按装配和温差后的工作游隙计算。With radial force, axial force and moment acting together, the relative displacement of the rings gives each ball’s load and operating contact angle; deep groove bearings are worked at the operating clearance after fitting and temperature.

接触与润滑Contact and lubrication

赫兹点接触用椭圆积分精确求解，给出最大接触应力和接触椭圆；检查椭圆是否越过挡肩；按 Hamrock–Dowson 公式算最小油膜厚度和 λ。Hertz point contact solved exactly with elliptic integrals gives the peak contact stress and the contact ellipse; the ellipse is checked against the shoulder; Hamrock–Dowson gives the minimum film thickness and λ.

缺数据就说“未知”Missing data says “unknown”

许用应力、最小壁厚等限值由企业按自己的标准填写；没有限值或缺数据的项显示“未知”，不会判为合格。额定载荷和寿命等国家标准正文录入后再算。Limits such as allowed stress and smallest wall come from your own standards; a check without its limit or data shows “unknown”, never a pass. Load ratings and life wait for the national standard texts to be entered.
