能做什么What it does

从运动要求出发Start from the motion

先定从动件什么时候升、升多少、停多久，再选运动规律；廓线由程序算出来，段与段之间速度、加速度连不连续当场标出。Say when the follower rises, how far and how long it dwells, then pick the motion laws; the profile is computed, and any jump in velocity or acceleration between segments is marked at once.

校核写得进计算书Checks that go in the calc sheet

合格、不合格、未知三种结论，全部由确定性程序计算；缺数据就是“未知”，不算合格。许用值由你填，软件不编。Pass, fail or unknown, all computed by deterministic code; missing data means unknown, never a pass. Allowable values come from you; the software does not invent them.

高速下跑不跑得住Will it hold at speed

给转速、质量、弹簧，算出不跳离的最高转速、凸轮上的力和驱动力矩；缝纫机、包装机这类高速凸轮最要紧的一项。Give speed, mass and spring and it finds the highest speed without jump, the force on the cam and the drive torque: what matters most for sewing and packaging machines.
