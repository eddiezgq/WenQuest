示例对话Example exchange
我把 Kd 调到 0 以后，摆一直来回晃，最后倒了。为什么？With Kd at 0 the pole kept swinging back and forth, then fell. Why?
你的曲线里振荡幅度一次比一次大，这是阻尼不足的典型现象。Kp 负责“拉回来”，但没有东西抵消回摆时的速度。想一想：哪一项是对角速度起作用的？试着把它加回来，每次改一点，观察振荡次数怎么变。 Your trace shows each swing growing larger, the signature of too little damping. Kp pulls the pole back, but nothing counters its speed on the way through. Which term acts on angular velocity? Add it back in small steps and watch how the number of oscillations changes. 出处：第 2 讲 PID 控制 · 2.3 微分项的作用Source: Lecture 2, PID control · 2.3 The derivative term
