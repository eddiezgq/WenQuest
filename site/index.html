III

数字化工厂Digital factory

一座按真实企业数据运转的教学工厂：物料、工艺路线、工位、质检都在系统里。学生在这里学排产、成本和质量，而不是只看流程图。A teaching factory running on realistic company data: materials, routings, workstations and inspection all live in the system. Students learn scheduling, cost and quality by running it, not by reading flowcharts.

IV

机器人虚拟实验室Virtual robotics labs

物理仿真直接在浏览器里运行，调参、失败、重来只要几秒。AI 助教读你的实验曲线，指出现象和原因，但不代你完成。Physics runs right in your browser, so tuning, failing and retrying take seconds. The AI tutor reads your traces and explains what happened, without doing the work for you.

倒立摆方程已与 MuJoCo 3.4 逐步对照；仿真里通过的控制器，才进入实体实验室的真机部署队列。The cart-pole equations are checked step by step against MuJoCo 3.4; only controllers that pass in simulation are queued for the physical lab.
