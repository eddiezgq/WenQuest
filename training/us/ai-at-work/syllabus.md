## Learning objectives

By the end of the course, each participant will be able to:

1. Explain in plain terms how a generative AI model produces text, and name its three most common failure modes (made-up details, out-of-date information, arithmetic and unit slips).
2. Write a five-part prompt (role and reader, facts and sources, outcome, format, limits) that produces a usable first draft of a real work document.
3. Verify AI output using the right check for each claim: primary source, recalculation, opening the cited source, or the responsible owner.
4. Apply the company's data rules: decide what may and may not be entered into an AI tool (public, internal, customer-confidential, export-controlled, personal data, credentials).
5. Use AI to draft and edit email, reports and procedures; to analyze and chart spreadsheet data; and to support engineering tasks (specification summaries, calculation cross-checks, simple scripts) with documented verification.
6. Build one repeatable AI-assisted workflow for their own job, and contribute to a one-page team AI use policy.

## Assessment and completion

- Labs completed in each session (recorded on the platform).
- Final quiz on the platform: 20 questions, pass mark 80 %, up to 3 attempts.
- Capstone: one documented AI-assisted workflow from the participant's own job, presented in Session 8.
- **Certificate of completion** requires attendance at 7 of 8 sessions, the final quiz passed, and the capstone presented.

Attendance (Zoom attendance report), lab completion and quiz scores are kept per participant and supplied to the employer for training-grant reporting.
