First they need to know: how long does one swing take? Does it depend on how heavy the housing is, or on how far it swings? We'll answer that at the end.

### 第 4 页 · 流程（chain）

- 标题：从现象到定律 / From phenomena to laws
- 流程：观察现象 → 提出假设 → 写成定律 → 推出预言 → 实验检验 / Observe → Hypothesise → State a law → Predict → Test
- 脚注：伽利略看吊灯（经验规律）→ 惠更斯《摆钟论》1673（周期公式）→ 牛顿 1687（用运动定律解释） / Galileo's lamp (empirical rule) → Huygens 1673 (period formula) → Newton 1687 (explained by the laws of motion)

讲稿：
- 物理学的方法可以粗略地概括为一个循环：观察现象，提出假设，用数学写成定律，由定律推出新的预言，再用实验检验。吊挂物体的摆动就走过这条路：传说伽利略看着比萨大教堂的吊灯摆动，发现摆幅不大时摆一次的时间几乎不变，这是经验规律；惠更斯在 1673 年的《摆钟论》中导出了周期公式；牛顿在 1687 年用运动定律说明了它为什么成立。物理定律有两个特点：它是普遍的，对吊灯、壳体、钟摆都成立；它有适用范围，在接近光速或原子尺度时要换成相对论和量子力学。
- The method of physics is roughly a cycle: observe, hypothesise, write the law in mathematics, predict something new, and test it. The swinging of a hanging object went exactly this way. Galileo, the story goes, watched a lamp in Pisa Cathedral and found that for small swings each swing takes almost the same time: an empirical rule. Huygens derived the period formula in his 1673 book on pendulum clocks, and Newton explained it with his laws of motion in 1687. Physical laws are universal: the same law covers the lamp, the housing and the clock. And they have a range of validity: near the speed of light or at atomic scales, relativity and quantum mechanics take over.

### 第 5 页 · 左文右图（split）

- 标题：模型：只保留主要因素 / A model keeps only what matters
- 要点：
  - 壳体 → 质点；钢丝绳 → 不伸长、没有质量的轻绳 / Housing → a point mass; rope → light and inextensible
  - 忽略空气阻力，再假定摆角很小 / Ignore air drag, and assume small swings
  - 得到单摆公式：周期与质量、摆幅都无关 / This gives the pendulum formula: the period depends on neither mass nor amplitude
- 画面：公式 $T_0 = 2\pi\sqrt{L/g}$（式 1.1.1）

讲稿：
- 真实的吊挂物体很复杂：壳体有大小和形状，钢丝绳有质量、会伸长，空气有阻力，小车还在动。要计算，就得把它简化成模型：壳体看成质点，绳子看成不伸长、没有质量的轻绳，忽略空气阻力，再假定摆角很小，就得到单摆的周期公式 T₀ 等于 2π 根号 L 比 g。它告诉我们：周期只和绳长、重力加速度有关，与质量无关，与摆幅也无关。
- A real hanging load is complicated. The housing has size and shape, the rope has mass and stretches, there is air drag, and the trolley moves. To calculate anything, we simplify it into a model: the housing is a point mass, the rope is light and does not stretch, air drag is ignored, and the swing is small. That gives the pendulum formula: T-nought equals two pi root L over g. The period depends only on the length and on g, not on the mass, and not on the amplitude.

### 第 6 页 · 图（figure）

- 标题：“小摆角”这个近似有多准？ / How good is the small-angle approximation?
- 画面：图 1.1.1 小摆角公式的相对误差随摆角的变化
- 要点：
  - 绳长 1.5 m：T₀ ≈ 2.46 s / Rope 1.5 m: T₀ ≈ 2.46 s
  - 摆角 10°：真实周期长 0.19%；30°：长 1.7%；60°：长 7.3% / At 10° the true period is 0.19% longer; at 30°, 1.7%; at 60°, 7.3%
  - 要求误差小于 1%，摆角不超过约 23° / For errors under 1%, keep the swing under about 23°

讲稿：
- 绳长 1.5 米，按公式算，周期约 2.46 秒。不作小摆角近似时，周期也能精确算出来，程序 1.1.1 用完全椭圆积分算了：摆角 10 度时真实周期只长 0.19%，30 度时长 1.7%，60 度时长 7.3%。图上可以读出：要求误差小于 1%，摆角不能超过约 23 度；要求小于 10%，可以放宽到 69 度左右。搬运时壳体的摆角一般在 10 度以内，公式足够准确。
- With a 1.5 m rope the formula gives a period of about 2.46 seconds. Without the small-angle approximation the period can still be computed exactly; program 1.1.1 does it with a complete elliptic integral. At 10 degrees the true period is only 0.19 percent longer; at 30 degrees, 1.7 percent; at 60 degrees, 7.3 percent. From the graph: for errors under 1 percent, keep the angle below about 23 degrees; for under 10 percent, about 69 degrees will do. A carried housing rarely swings more than 10 degrees, so the formula is accurate enough.

### 第 7 页 · 要点（points）

- 标题：好模型“刚好够用” / A good model is just good enough
- 要点：
  - 每个近似都有成立的条件：质点、轻绳、无阻力、小摆角 / Every approximation has conditions: point mass, light rope, no drag, small angle
  - 太粗，答案不可靠；太细，算不动，也看不清规律 / Too crude and the answer is unreliable; too detailed and you can't compute or see the pattern
  - 用公式前先问前提：它在你的问题里成立吗？ / Before using a formula, ask whether its assumptions hold in your problem

讲稿：
- 这个例子说明了模型和近似的关系。“摆角很小”是近似，它能不能用，取决于要求的精度。“质点”“轻绳”“没有空气阻力”也都是近似，各有成立的条件。好的模型抓住主要因素，并且清楚自己在哪里会失效。工程师的基本功之一，就是为问题选一个刚好够用的模型。手册上的公式几乎都带着隐含的前提，用之前先问一句：这些前提在我的问题里成立吗？
- This shows how models and approximations relate. "Small angle" is an approximation, and whether it is good enough depends on the accuracy you need. "Point mass", "light rope" and "no air drag" are approximations too, each with its own conditions. A good model captures the main factors and knows where it fails. One basic skill of an engineer is choosing a model that is just good enough. Handbook formulas almost always carry hidden assumptions, so ask first: do they hold in my problem?

### 第 9 页 · 大数字（bignum）

- 标题：单位弄错的代价 / The cost of a unit mix-up
- 大数字：
  - 1999：火星气候探测者号进入火星轨道时烧毁 / The Mars Climate Orbiter burned up on arrival
  - 4.45 倍：同一个数，按 lbf·s 理解是按 N·s 的 4.45 倍 / The same number means 4.45 times more in lbf·s than in N·s
- 脚注：地面软件按磅力·秒输出推进器冲量，导航软件当作牛顿·秒使用，偏差累积了九个多月 / Ground software reported thruster impulse in lbf·s; navigation read it as N·s; the error built up for over nine months

讲稿：
- 单位弄错的代价可以很大。1999 年 9 月，美国的火星气候探测者号在进入火星轨道时坠毁。调查发现，地面一个软件按磅力·秒输出推进器喷气的冲量，导航软件却把它当成牛顿·秒。1 磅力约 4.448 牛，导航软件用到的冲量只有真实值的约 1/4.45。每次喷气的误差都很小，可九个多月累积下来，探测器飞得离火星太近，在大气中烧毁了。
- Getting units wrong can be very expensive. In September 1999 NASA's Mars Climate Orbiter was lost as it arrived at Mars. One ground program reported thruster impulse in pound-force seconds, but the navigation software took the numbers as newton seconds. One pound-force is about 4.448 newtons, so navigation used only about one 4.45th of the real impulse. Each error was small, but over nine months they added up; the probe flew too close and burned up in the atmosphere.

### 第 10 页 · 左文右图（split）

- 标题：七个基本单位 / Seven base units
- 要点：
  - 物理量 = 数值 × 单位，例如 L = 0.85 m / Quantity = number × unit, e.g. L = 0.85 m
  - 秒 s、米 m、千克 kg、安培 A、开尔文 K、摩尔 mol、坎德拉 cd / s, m, kg, A, K, mol, cd
  - 其他都是导出单位：1 N = 1 kg·m/s² / Everything else is derived: 1 N = 1 kg·m/s²
- 画面：图 1.2.1 七个定义常数与七个基本单位

讲稿：
- 物理量等于数值乘以单位，比如 UR5e 的工作半径 L 等于 0.85 米。选定少数几个量的单位作为基本单位，其他单位都可以由公式导出。国际单位制选了七个：秒、米、千克、安培、开尔文、摩尔、坎德拉。比如力的单位牛顿由 F = ma 导出，1 牛等于 1 千克米每二次方秒。我国的法定计量单位以国际单位制为基础，本书一律使用它。
- A physical quantity is a number times a unit: the UR5e's reach L is 0.85 metres. Pick the units of a few quantities as base units, and all other units follow from formulas. The SI picks seven: the second, metre, kilogram, ampere, kelvin, mole and candela. The newton, for example, comes from F = ma: one newton is one kilogram metre per second squared. China's legal units are based on the SI, and this book uses SI throughout.

### 第 11 页 · 图（figure）

- 标题：2019 年起，用常数定义单位 / Since 2019, units are defined by constants
- 画面：图 1.2.1；表 1.2.1 七个定义常数
- 要点：
  - 实物会变：国际千克原器和复制品一百年相差约 50 μg / Artefacts drift: the kilogram prototype and its copies differed by about 50 μg in a century
  - 规定光速 c = 299 792 458 m/s 精确，米就随之确定 / Fix c = 299 792 458 m/s exactly, and the metre follows
  - 规定普朗克常量 h 的数值，千克就随之确定 / Fix the value of Planck's constant h, and the kilogram follows

讲稿：
- 一百三十年里，千克就是巴黎附近一块铂铱合金圆柱的质量。可是实物会变：国际千克原器和它的几十个复制品定期比对，一百年间相差了约 50 微克，谁也说不清是谁变了。办法是用自然界的常数来定义单位。2019 年 5 月 20 日起，七个基本单位全部由七个数值精确规定的常数确定。思路是倒过来的：过去先有米，再测光速；现在规定光速恰好是 299 792 458 米每秒，米就是光在这段时间的倒数里走的距离。千克由普朗克常量定义。这些数值选得与旧定义衔接，你的体重不会因此改变。
- For 130 years the kilogram was the mass of a platinum-iridium cylinder near Paris. But objects change: the prototype and its dozens of copies drifted apart by about 50 micrograms in a century, and no one could say which had changed. The cure is to define units by constants of nature. Since 20 May 2019 all seven base units are fixed by seven constants with exact values. The logic is reversed: we used to have the metre and measure the speed of light; now the speed of light is exactly 299,792,458 metres per second, and the metre is the distance light travels in one 299,792,458th of a second. The kilogram is fixed by Planck's constant. The values were chosen to match the old definitions, so your weight did not change.

### 第 12 页 · 要点（points）

- 标题：单位怎么写、怎么换算 / Writing and converting units
- 要点：
  - 5 kg 中间空一格；千克是小写 kg；不写 mμg，写 ng / Write 5 kg with a space; kilogram is lower-case kg; ng, not mμg
  - 20 kgf·cm ≈ 1.961 N·m（kgf、rpm 不是法定单位） / 20 kgf·cm ≈ 1.961 N·m (kgf and rpm are not legal units)
  - 180 °/s = π rad/s ≈ 3.1416 rad/s，力学公式里角度用弧度 / 180 °/s = π rad/s ≈ 3.1416 rad/s; use radians in mechanics

讲稿：
- 书写单位要按国家标准：数值和单位之间空一格，千克写小写的 kg，词头不能叠用。手册里常见的 kgf·cm、rpm 不是法定单位，计算前先换算。小舵机的堵转扭矩 20 千克力厘米，1 千克力是 9.806 65 牛，所以约 1.961 牛米。UR5e 的关节最大速度 180 度每秒，就是 π 弧度每秒，约 3.1416 弧度每秒。在力学公式里，角度和角速度一律用弧度。在程序里，最好只用国际单位计算，变量名带上单位。
- Follow the national standard when writing units: a space between number and unit, lower-case kg, no stacked prefixes. Handbook units like kgf·cm and rpm are not legal units; convert them before calculating. A small servo's stall torque of 20 kgf·cm is about 1.961 newton metres, since one kilogram-force is 9.80665 newtons. The UR5e's maximum joint speed of 180 degrees per second is pi radians per second, about 3.1416. In mechanics, always use radians. In code, compute only in SI and put the unit in the variable name.

### 第 14 页 · 要点（points）

- 标题：量纲：物理量由什么组成 / Dimension: what a quantity is made of
- 要点：
  - dim Q = LᵅMᵝTᵞ：速度 LT⁻¹，力 MLT⁻²，能量 ML²T⁻² / dim Q = LᵅMᵝTᵞ: velocity LT⁻¹, force MLT⁻², energy ML²T⁻²
  - 只有量纲相同的量才能相加、相等：方程必须量纲齐次 / Only quantities of the same dimension can be added or equated
  - 推完公式先查量纲，是发现错误最便宜的办法 / Checking dimensions is the cheapest way to catch errors

讲稿：
- 量纲表示一个物理量由哪些基本量、以怎样的幂次组成，与用什么单位无关。力学里用长度 L、质量 M、时间 T 三个基本量：速度是 L T 的负一次方，力是 M L T 的负二次方，能量是 M L 平方 T 负二次方。基本规则是：只有量纲相同的量才能相加、相减、相等。所以推导完一个公式，先看量纲对不对：量纲不对的一定错；量纲对的不一定对，但已经排除了很多错误。三角函数、指数、对数的自变量也必须是量纲一的。
- The dimension of a quantity says which base quantities it is made of, and to which powers, regardless of units. Mechanics uses three: length L, mass M and time T. Velocity is L T to the minus one, force is M L T to the minus two, energy M L squared T to the minus two. The rule: only quantities of the same dimension can be added, subtracted or set equal. So after deriving a formula, check its dimensions. If they are wrong, it's wrong; if they're right, it may still be wrong, but many errors are ruled out. Arguments of sines, exponentials and logarithms must be dimensionless.

### 第 15 页 · 公式（formula）

- 标题：不解方程，猜出单摆公式 / Guessing the pendulum formula without solving anything
- 公式：$T = C\,m^a L^b g^c \;\Rightarrow\; \mathsf T = \mathsf M^a\mathsf L^{b+c}\mathsf T^{-2c} \;\Rightarrow\; a = 0,\ b = \tfrac12,\ c = -\tfrac12$
- 要点：
  - 周期与质量无关，∝ √(L/g)：摆长变 4 倍，周期加倍 / Period independent of mass, ∝ √(L/g): four times the length doubles it
  - 常数 C = 2π 要靠理论或实验 / The constant C = 2π needs theory or experiment
  - 漏掉摆角 θ₀，就看不出大摆角时周期变长 / Leave out θ₀ and you miss the longer period at large angles

讲稿：
- 量纲齐次性还可以反过来用。设单摆周期只与质量 m、摆长 L、重力加速度 g 有关，写成 T 等于 C 乘 m 的 a 次、L 的 b 次、g 的 c 次。两边量纲相同，比较 M、L、T 的指数，得 a 等于零，b 等于二分之一，c 等于负二分之一。没有解任何方程，就知道周期与质量无关，与根号 L 比 g 成正比。量纲分析也有局限：它定不出常数 C，C = 2π 要靠理论；它依赖于我们列全了有关的量，漏掉摆角，就看不出大摆角时周期会变长。
- Dimensional homogeneity works backwards too. Suppose the period depends only on the mass m, the length L and g: T equals C times m to the a, L to the b, g to the c. Match the powers of M, L and T on both sides: a is zero, b is one half, c is minus one half. Without solving any equation, we know the period is independent of mass and proportional to root L over g. The method has limits. It cannot find the constant C; that two pi comes from theory. And it relies on listing every relevant quantity: leave out the swing angle and you'll never see the period grow at large angles.

### 第 16 页 · 左文右图（split）

- 标题：人和机器狗，多快由走变跑？ / Walking to running: people and robot dogs
- 要点：
  - 弗劳德数 Fr = v²/(gL)，人和许多动物在 Fr ≈ 0.5 时由走变跑 / Froude number Fr = v²/(gL); people and many animals switch at about 0.5
  - 腿长 0.9 m 的人约 2.1 m/s；腿长 0.28 m 的 Go2 约 1.2 m/s / A person (0.9 m legs) about 2.1 m/s; a Go2 (0.28 m) about 1.2 m/s
  - Go2 平时用小跑步态，所以能快得多 / A Go2 normally trots, so it can go much faster
- 画面：图 1.3.1 走跑转换速度随腿长的变化

讲稿：
- 走路时，支撑腿像一根倒立的摆，身体在腿的上方划过一段圆弧，需要的向心加速度 v 平方比 L 只能由重力提供，不能超过 g。所以决定走路方式的是量纲一的组合 v 平方比 gL，叫弗劳德数。人和许多四足动物都在弗劳德数约 0.5 时由走变跑，与身体大小无关。腿长 0.9 米的人，转换速度约 2.1 米每秒，与实测相符；宇树 Go2 腿长 0.28 米，按倒立摆式的“走”最快约 1.2 米每秒。实际上 Go2 平时用小跑步态，对角的两条腿同时着地，像压在弹簧上，不受这个限制，所以能跑得快得多。
- When you walk, the stance leg is an inverted pendulum and your body swings over it on an arc. The centripetal acceleration v squared over L can only come from gravity, so it can't exceed g. The deciding quantity is therefore the dimensionless group v squared over g L, the Froude number. People and many four-legged animals switch from walking to running at a Froude number of about 0.5, whatever their size. A person with 0.9 m legs switches at about 2.1 metres per second, as measured. A Unitree Go2, with legs of 0.28 m, could only "walk" at about 1.2 metres per second. In fact the Go2 normally trots, with diagonal legs landing together like a mass on a spring, so the limit doesn't apply and it goes much faster.

Different branches of physics live at different scales: nuclear, atomic, everyday mechanics, astrophysics. The scale of a phenomenon often decides which theory describes it.

### 第 19 页 · 流程（chain）

- 标题：费米问题：几分钟估个数 / Fermi problems: a number in minutes
- 流程：把问题拆成几个因素 → 每个因素取一两位数 → 拿不准取几何平均 → 相乘，结果写“约” / Split into factors → one or two digits each → geometric mean when unsure → multiply, say "about"
- 脚注：1945 年费米撒碎纸片，当场估出爆炸当量，与精确分析在同一数量级 / In 1945 Fermi dropped scraps of paper and estimated the blast yield on the spot, to the right order of magnitude

讲稿：
- 1945 年第一颗原子弹试爆时，费米在约 16 公里外把碎纸片从手中撒下，看冲击波把纸片吹出多远，当场估出了爆炸当量，与后来仔细分析的结果在同一数量级。这类只凭常识和少量数据推出数量级的问题，叫费米问题。做法是把问题拆成几个容易估的因素相乘，每个因素只取一两位数；拿不准时取合理范围两端的几何平均，比如在 10 到 1000 之间就取 100。各因素的误差往往部分抵消，结果常常落在正确的数量级里。
- At the first atomic test in 1945, Fermi, about 16 kilometres away, dropped scraps of paper and watched how far the blast wave carried them. On the spot he estimated the yield, to the same order of magnitude as the careful analysis later. Problems like this, where common sense and a few data give an order of magnitude, are called Fermi problems. Split the problem into factors that are easy to estimate and multiply them, keeping one or two digits each. When unsure, take the geometric mean of a sensible range: between 10 and 1000, take 100. The errors tend to cancel, and the answer usually lands in the right order of magnitude.

### 第 4 页 · 左文右图（split）

- 标题：随机误差与系统误差 / Random and systematic errors
- 要点：
  - 随机误差：每次大小正负都变，多测取平均可以减小 / Random errors change every time; averaging reduces them
  - 系统误差：每次都一样，多测没用，要校准、修正 / Systematic errors stay the same; only calibration and correction help
  - 精密 ≠ 准确：UR5e“重复定位精度 ±0.03 mm”说的是精密度 / Precise ≠ accurate: the UR5e's "repeatability ±0.03 mm" is about precision
- 画面：图 1.5.1 准确度与精密度（打靶）

讲稿：
- 测量结果和真值之差叫误差，分两类。随机误差在重复测量时大小和正负都随机变化，比如估读的偏差、量爪每次放的位置略有不同，多测几次取平均可以减小它。系统误差在重复测量时保持不变，比如卡尺零点没对准、温度不是 20 度引起的热膨胀，多测没有用，只能校准和修正。与此对应，精密度说的是重复测量彼此接近，准确度说的是接近真值。看这张打靶图：读数很集中，却集中在偏离靶心的地方，就是精密而不准确。UR5e 规格里的重复定位精度 ±0.03 毫米说的是精密度，离程序里目标点有多远是另一回事。
- The difference between a measurement and the true value is the error, and there are two kinds. Random errors change in size and sign each time: a slightly different estimate, the jaws placed slightly differently. Averaging repeated readings reduces them. Systematic errors stay the same each time: a caliper whose zero is off, thermal expansion when it isn't 20 degrees. Repeating doesn't help; only calibration and correction do. Correspondingly, precision means repeated readings agree, accuracy means they're close to the truth. In this target picture, the shots are tightly grouped but off-centre: precise, not accurate. The UR5e's repeatability of plus or minus 0.03 mm describes precision; how far it lands from a programmed target is another matter.

### 第 5 页 · 要点（points）

- 标题：不确定度与有效数字 / Uncertainty and significant figures
- 要点：
  - 真值不知道，所以说“分散有多大”：标准不确定度 u，扩展不确定度 U / The true value is unknown, so we state the spread: standard uncertainty u, expanded uncertainty U
  - 卡尺读数写 62.96 mm，不写 62.960 mm / Write a caliper reading as 62.96 mm, not 62.960 mm
  - 修约“四舍六入五成双”：2.6950 → 2.70 / Round half to even: 2.6950 → 2.70

讲稿：
- 既然真值不知道，误差也就无法确切知道。现代计量学不再说误差是多少，而是说测量结果有多大的分散，这就是不确定度：用标准差表示的叫标准不确定度 u，乘以一个因子给出一个区间的叫扩展不确定度 U。写数字也有规矩：卡尺读数 62.96 毫米，最后一位已经有误差，写成 62.960 就暗示能分辨到千分之一毫米，是错的。修约按国家标准“四舍六入五成双”，比如 2.6950 保留三位是 2.70。
- Since we don't know the true value, we can't know the error exactly either. Modern metrology states instead how widely the result may be spread: that's uncertainty. Expressed as a standard deviation it's the standard uncertainty u; multiplied by a factor to give an interval, it's the expanded uncertainty U. Numbers have rules too. A caliper reading of 62.96 mm already has an uncertain last digit; writing 62.960 implies you can resolve a thousandth of a millimetre, which is wrong. Round by the national standard, half to even: 2.6950 to three figures is 2.70.

### 第 6 页 · 公式（formula）

- 标题：A 类评定：用统计方法 / Type A: by statistics
- 公式：$\bar x = \dfrac1n\sum x_i ,\quad s = \sqrt{\dfrac{\sum(x_i - \bar x)^2}{n - 1}} ,\quad u_\mathrm A = \dfrac{s}{\sqrt n}$（式 1.5.1–1.5.3）
- 要点：
  - 平均值是最佳估计；分母 n − 1：偏差之和恒为零 / The mean is the best estimate; n − 1 because the deviations sum to zero
  - 只按 1/√n 改善：测 4 次好一倍，测 100 次才好十倍 / Improves only as 1/√n: 4 readings twice as good, 100 readings ten times

讲稿：
- 不确定度有两类评定方法。A 类用统计方法处理重复测量的数据。测 n 次，平均值是最佳估计；单次读数的分散用实验标准差 s 表示，分母是 n 减 1，因为各读数与平均值之差的和恒为零，只有 n 减 1 个是独立的。平均值的分散只有单次的根号 n 分之一，所以平均值的 A 类不确定度是 s 除以根号 n。注意它改善得很慢：测 4 次比 1 次好一倍，要测 100 次才好十倍。
- There are two ways to evaluate an uncertainty. Type A uses statistics on repeated readings. With n readings, the mean is the best estimate. The spread of single readings is the experimental standard deviation s, with n minus one in the denominator, because the deviations from the mean always add to zero, so only n minus one are independent. The mean spreads only one over root n as much as a single reading, so its type A uncertainty is s over root n. It improves slowly: four readings are twice as good as one, but you need a hundred to be ten times better.

### 第 7 页 · 公式（formula）

- 标题：B 类评定，合成与扩展 / Type B, combining and expanding
- 公式：$u_\mathrm B = \dfrac{a}{\sqrt3} ,\quad u_\mathrm c = \sqrt{u_\mathrm A^2 + u_\mathrm B^2} ,\quad U = k\,u_\mathrm c,\ k = 2$（式 1.5.4–1.5.6）
- 要点：
  - B 类：用最大允许误差 ±a 等信息，均匀分布时 a/√3 / Type B: from information such as a maximum permissible error ±a; a/√3 for a uniform distribution
  - 独立来源按平方和合成，不是直接相加 / Independent sources add in quadrature, not directly
  - 结果写成 x = (x̄ ± U) 单位（k = 2），U 取一两位有效数字 / Report x = (x̄ ± U) unit (k = 2), with U to one or two figures

讲稿：
- B 类用统计以外的信息，比如检定规程规定的最大允许误差。只知道误差在正负 a 以内、各处可能性相同时，标准不确定度是 a 除以根号 3。几个独立的来源按平方和合成，得合成标准不确定度；注意不是直接相加，两个一样大的独立误差合起来只放大到根号 2 倍，因为它们有时互相抵消。合成不确定度只相当于一个标准差，报告时乘以包含因子 k，本书一律取 2，得扩展不确定度 U。结果写成平均值加减 U、单位、括号注明 k 等于 2；U 取一两位有效数字，测量值修约到和 U 的末位对齐。
- Type B uses information other than statistics, such as the maximum permissible error in a calibration standard. If all you know is that the error lies within plus or minus a, equally likely anywhere, the standard uncertainty is a over root three. Independent contributions add in quadrature to give the combined standard uncertainty. Not directly: two equal independent errors only make root two times one, because they partly cancel. The combined uncertainty is one standard deviation; for the report we multiply by a coverage factor k, always 2 in this book, to get the expanded uncertainty U. ### 第 24 页 · 左文右图（split）

- 标题：生活中的例子：厨房秤 / Everyday example: a kitchen scale
- 要点：
  - 显示到 1 g，说明书写“精度 ±2 g”，称 100 g 面粉 / Displays 1 g, spec says "accuracy ±2 g"; weigh 100 g of flour
  - 示值误差约 2/√3 ≈ 1.2 g，读数舍入约 0.3 g，合成约 1.2 g / Indication error ≈ 2/√3 ≈ 1.2 g, rounding ≈ 0.3 g, combined ≈ 1.2 g
  - 写成 (100 ± 3) g（k = 2）：做蛋糕够了，配药就不行 / Report (100 ± 3) g (k = 2): fine for a cake, not for medicine

讲稿：
- 一个生活中的例子。厨房电子秤显示到 1 克，说明书写着精度正负 2 克，称 100 克面粉，结果怎么写？示值误差按均匀分布，标准不确定度约 2 除以根号 3，1.2 克；读数舍入约 0.3 克；合成约 1.2 克，扩展不确定度约 2.4 克，不确定度只进不舍，写成 100 加减 3 克。做蛋糕足够了，配药就不行。同样的思路，也可以用来判断体重秤上零点几千克的变化是不是真的。
- An everyday example. A kitchen scale shows 1 gram and its manual says accuracy plus or minus 2 grams. How do you report 100 grams of flour? The indication error, uniformly distributed, gives about 2 over root three, 1.2 grams; rounding gives about 0.3; combined about 1.2, expanded about 2.4, rounded up to 3. So: 100 plus or minus 3 grams. Fine for a cake, not for medicine. The same thinking tells you whether a few tenths of a kilogram on the bathroom scale mean anything.

讲稿：
- 今天讲了测量。测量都有误差：系统误差使结果偏向一边，随机误差使结果分散，精密不等于准确。测量结果用不确定度表示可信程度：A 类用统计，平均值的标准不确定度是 s 除以根号 n；B 类由最大允许误差等信息评定，均匀分布时是 a 除以根号 3；按平方和合成，乘以 2 得扩展不确定度，结果写成平均值加减 U、k 等于 2。处理数据时先作图、必要时线性化，再用最小二乘法求斜率、截距和它们的不确定度，看残差图检查模型；用斜率求物理量能避开某些系统误差。课后做五道习题，完成两个实验的报告和第 2 讲的测验。下一章开始，我们补齐矢量这个数学工具。
- Today was about measurement. Every measurement has errors: systematic ones push the result one way, random ones scatter it, and precise is not the same as accurate. Uncertainty expresses how far to trust a result. Type A by statistics: the mean's standard uncertainty is s over root n. Type B from information such as the maximum permissible error: a over root three for a uniform distribution. Add in quadrature, multiply by two for the expanded uncertainty, and report the mean plus or minus U with k equals 2. With data, graph first, linearise if needed, then use least squares for the slope, intercept and their uncertainties, and check the residuals; using a slope can dodge some systematic errors.
