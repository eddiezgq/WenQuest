### What you will be able to do {#l19-goal}

<ul>
<li>Create an analysis: FEM workbench, Model → New Analysis.</li>
<li>Constraints and loads: fixed (faces), force (faces, magnitude and direction), centrifugal (solid, axis and speed).</li>
<li>Compute and check: no mesh or solver setup; for a hand-built analysis pick a library material first (values with sources).</li>
<li>Read the checks: reaction balance, mesh quality, convergence of max stress and max displacement; if any fails, no safety factor is given.</li>
<li>Report: language, number, revision; a Word file, plus PDF when LibreOffice is installed.</li>
</ul>

### Hand-built analysis, compute and check {#l19-run}

::video{src=wqcad-l19a-en title="Screen recording: FEM workbench, new analysis → fix the shaft bore → crank-pin force 752.5 N along the ForceDirection line → centrifugal: whole part, about the SpinAxis line, 83.333 Hz → Compute and check, pick 45-QT → results (about 3 min; the computing wait is cut)"}

- Where the force comes from: lesson 16 (WenQuest MBD) found a crank-pin force peaking at about 752 N at 5000 rpm. Here it is applied once in the worst direction (pin centre toward shaft centre) — a conservative static check.
- The two helper lines are already in the file: ForceDirection runs from the pin centre to the shaft centre; SpinAxis is the upper-shaft axis. For the force direction, select the line first, then click Direction in the task panel.
- The centrifugal frequency is in Hz (revolutions per second): 5000 rpm ÷ 60 = 83.333 Hz. Set the selection mode to Solid so the whole part carries the load.
- Narrow hole faces are hard to click: rotate the view first, or pick in the tree. If you hit an edge or vertex, Remove it and add again.
- Material 45-QT (steel 45, quenched and tempered), yield strength 355 MPa. Library values have sources, listed in the report; FEM's own material cards are not used.
- Result in this lesson: max von Mises stress about 8.4 MPa, safety factor about 42; across three mesh levels max stress differs by 0.21 % and max displacement by 0.24 %; all four checks pass. The crank has plenty of strength — to lighten it or use aluminium, change it and compute again.
- Computing takes a minute or two depending on the computer; you can keep working meanwhile.

### Report {#l19-report}

::video{src=wqcad-l19b-en title="Screen recording: select the analysis → Solve → Report (parts library) → language, number WQ-FEA-019, revision A → no AI explanation → Word and PDF → page through (about 90 s)"}

- Sections: summary, study (part, material and source, constraints, loads, steps), mesh and trust checks, result plots, interpretation and advice (only when AI drafts it), limitations, review checklist, traceability (program versions, file fingerprints, revision history).
- Having AI write the interpretation is optional: all numbers come from the program, AI never decides pass/fail, and an engineer must judge what it writes.
- If you change constraints or loads after computing, the report is refused: run Compute and check again first.
- Reports are a Pro feature; the free edition computes and shows results and checks.

### Draft from one sentence (AI) {#l19-ai}

::video{src=wqcad-l19c-en title="Screen recording: WenQuest FEA example 02, shoulder hollow shaft RJ-201 → Draft from one sentence (AI, recorded replay) → read the questions, confirm the study → Compute and check: max stress does not converge, overall “fail”, no safety factor (about 2 min; the computing wait is cut)"}

- The sentence must give the part, material, how it is held and the loads (numbers and units). AI turns it into FEM constraints, loads and material; the program checks each item (faces found, units, material in the library). What the AI is unsure of is listed as questions; what the program rejects is listed as not adopted.
- A person reads the questions, fixes things in FEM or decides they are not needed, ticks “questions handled”, enters a name and clicks Confirm study. An unconfirmed AI draft cannot be computed.
- In the recording the AI reply is a recorded replay of a real model call (the panel says “replay”); the sentence is in Chinese because that is what was recorded. To use it yourself you need an AI key (Claude in the US, DeepSeek in China). Drafting from one sentence is a Pro feature.
- Result of this example: reaction balance, mesh quality and displacement convergence pass, but max stress keeps rising with refinement — a suspected singularity at the sharp corner of the step — so the overall verdict is “fail” and no safety factor is given. That is correct: a peak at a sharp corner is not a design basis; add a fillet or read the stress away from the corner.

### Follow along {#l19-do}

<ol class="steps">
<li>Open the course file NeedleBarCrank, switch to the FEM workbench, Model → New Analysis.</li>
<li>Model → Mechanical Boundary Conditions and Loads → Fixed Boundary Condition: Add, click the inside of the shaft bore (⌀17), OK.</li>
<li>Force Load: Add, click the crank-pin hole face; force 752.5 N; select the ForceDirection line, click Direction; OK.</li>
<li>Centrifugal Load: selection mode Solid, Add, click the crank; under the axis selector click Add and click the SpinAxis line; rotation frequency 83.333 Hz; OK.</li>
<li>Solve → Compute and check (parts library), pick 45-QT, wait; read the overall trust verdict in the message.</li>
<li>Look at the stress plot and compare: max about 8.4 MPa, safety factor about 42.</li>
<li>Select the analysis, Solve → Report (parts library), enter a number, no AI, open the Word / PDF.</li>
<li>(With an AI key) new file, import a part, select it, Solve → Draft from one sentence (AI), write a sentence like the one in the video, read the questions, confirm, compute.</li>
</ol>

### On your own {#l19-self}

1. Double the force (1505 N) and compute again: roughly what is the max stress now? (Linear static: doubling the force doubles the stress it causes; the centrifugal part stays.)
2. Switch the material to an aluminium alloy from the library and compare the safety factor.
3. Delete the centrifugal load, keep fixed and force, compute again and see how much the centrifugal load contributed.
