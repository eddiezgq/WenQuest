### What you will be able to do {#l07-goal}

<ul>
<li>Dress-up features (chamfer, fillet, draft, thickness) come after the shape is built: pick edges or faces first, then the tool.</li>
<li>Multiple selection: hold Ctrl to add a second or third edge.</li>
<li>Thickness: the selected faces are opened; every other face keeps the same wall.</li>
<li>Know the limits: Thickness is uniform; a fillet that is too large fails.</li>
</ul>

### Five dress-up features {#l07-five}

| Feature | Pick | Main parameters | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chamfer | edges | size (or two distances, or distance and angle) | shaft ends, hole edges: easy assembly, no sharp edges |
| Fillet | edges | radius | inside and outside corners of castings and mouldings; less stress concentration |
| Draft | faces + neutral plane | angle | castings and mouldings release from the mould (1°–3°) |
| Thickness | faces to open | thickness, inwards or outwards | cups, boxes, covers |
| Thicken (Part workbench, Offset → Thicken) | a surface or face | thickness | turning surfaces into solids (lesson 14) |

Do dress-up features last: finish the shape, then chamfer and fillet. If an earlier feature changes, edge numbers may change and the dress-up reports a missing edge — double-click it and pick the edges again.

### Bobbin case: thickness {#l07-shell}

The bobbin case is a small cup that holds the bobbin: 22.4 outside, 14 deep, 0.6 wall.

::video{src=wqcad-l07b-en title="Recording: circle 22.4 → Pad 14 → click the top face → Thickness 0.6 (about 55 s)"}

After Thickness the volume is {{l7.shell}} mm³. The real bobbin case in the machine is {{l7.real}} mm³ — its bottom is 1.5 thick, not 0.6. Thickness is the same everywhere; for a thin wall and a thick bottom, skip Thickness: sketch a Ø21.2 circle on the end face and Pocket 12.5 mm to cut the cavity (On your own, 1).

### Follow along {#l07-do}

<ol class="steps">
<li>Open last lesson's “NeedleBar”. Click the bottom edge, Ctrl-click the top edge, Chamfer 0.5. Check the volume.</li>
<li>New file: circle Ø22.4 on XY, Pad 14.</li>
<li>Click the top face, Thickness 0.6, OK.</li>
</ol>

### On your own {#l07-self}

1. Remake the bobbin case with a 1.5 bottom: after Pad 14, sketch a Ø21.2 circle on the top face and Pocket 12.5. The volume should be {{l7.real}} mm³.
2. Fillet both ends of the crank pin R0.5, then change to a 0.5 × 45° chamfer and compare the volumes.
3. In Thickness, untick “Make thickness inwards” (grow outwards). What does the outside diameter become?
