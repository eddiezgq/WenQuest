Two things are made in this lesson: the **presser-bar collar** (OD {{pc.od}}, bore {{pc.id}}, {{pc.t}} mm thick, exactly as in the WQ-LS design) and the **needle-clamp outline** (constraints and exact sizes come in the next lesson).

### What a sketch is {#l03-what}

The basic FreeCAD way to make a part: **first draw a 2D sketch on a plane, then turn the sketch into 3D** (pad, pocket, revolve…). A sketch holds two kinds of things:

- **Geometry**: lines, circles, arcs — the shape;
- **Constraints**: coincident, horizontal, vertical, diameter, distance — what pins the shape down.

While you edit a sketch, the “Sketch edit” panel always tells you how far you are: “Under-constrained: n degrees of freedom”, “Fully constrained” (all green) or “Over-constrained / redundant constraints” (red text). This small experiment imitates the two clicks of the circle tool: first the centre, then drag out the radius or type a diameter — watch how the constraints and degrees of freedom change.

### Recording: the needle-clamp outline {#l03-clamp}

The needle clamp sits at the bottom of the needle bar: an arc on top (radius {{nc.R}}), a square tab below (width {{nc.w}}, reaching {{nc.dep}} below the centre), and a bore in the middle for the needle bar (diameter {{nc.bore}}). In this lesson we only draw the shape; exact sizes come later:

<ol class="steps">
<li><b>Polyline</b>: top right → bottom right → bottom left → top left, four clicks; right-click to finish, Esc to leave. FreeCAD adds “Vertical” to the vertical lines, “Horizontal” to the horizontal one and “Coincident” where lines meet.</li>
<li><b>Arc</b> (centre, start, end): click the origin, then the top-right end point, go round over the top, click the top-left end point.</li>
<li><b>Circle</b>: centre at the origin, type diameter 8.</li>
<li><b>Line</b>: draw a vertical line from bottom to top, then click “Toggle construction geometry”: it turns into a blue dashed centre line, used only for positioning.</li>
</ol>

::video{src=wqcad-l03b-en title="Recording: polyline, arc, circle and construction line for the needle clamp (about 50 s)"}

When you finish, the panel says “Under-constrained: 6 degrees of freedom” — the shape is right, but it can still be dragged. The next lesson pins it down.

### Common sketch tools {#l03-tools}

| Tool | How to draw | What you can type |
|---|---|---|
| Line | Start point, end point | Length, angle |
| Polyline | Click point after point; right-click to finish; press M to switch line / arc segments | Each segment's length |
| Circle | Centre, a point on the circle | Diameter |
| Arc | Centre, start, end (counter-clockwise) | Radius, angle |
| Rectangle | Two opposite corners | Length, width |
| Construction | Draw, then “Toggle construction geometry” (or toggle before drawing) | — |

Typing numbers while drawing is “on-view parameters”: the numbers go into the small box next to the cursor, Enter confirms, and FreeCAD adds the matching dimensional constraint.
