### What you will be able to do {#l11-goal}

<ul>
<li>Create primitives and set their size and position in the properties.</li>
<li>Booleans: Cut (A − B), Fuse (A + B), Common (A ∩ B); the selection order decides what is cut from what.</li>
<li>3D Offset: grow (or shrink) a whole shape by a layer.</li>
<li>Section and cross-sections: the intersection curve of two shapes, or a set of slices along a plane.</li>
</ul>

### Part or Part Design? {#l11-which}

| | Part workbench | Part Design workbench |
|---|---|---|
| Approach | combine and subtract shapes (constructive solid geometry, CSG) | one solid, built feature by feature |
| Starts from | primitives, extrude, revolve | sketches |
| Good for | simple parts made of regular solids; jigs and fixtures; Booleans and offsets on existing shapes | most mechanical parts: constraints, parameters, drawings |
| Result | may be several solids | one solid per Body |

They work together: Part Design's additive/subtractive primitives are Part primitives, and Part shapes can be brought into Part Design as a base feature.

### Feed eccentric: two cylinders and a cut {#l11-ecc}

The WQ-LS feed eccentric: a Ø26 disc 8 thick whose centre is 3 off the shaft axis, with a Ø16 bore on the lower shaft.

::video{src=wqcad-l11a-en title="Recording: cylinder R13 × 8 at x = 3 → cylinder R8 × 8 → Cut → 3D Offset 1 (about 80 s)"}

After the cut the volume is {{l11.cut}} mm³, exactly the eccentric in the machine STEP (there the offset points in a slanted direction, but it is also 3). With a 1 mm 3D offset it is {{l11.off}} mm³.

### Booleans {#l11-bool}

| Operation | Selection | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Cut | the part to cut first, then Ctrl-click the tool | the first minus the second |
| Fuse | two or more | one shape; tick Refine to remove extra seams |
| Common | two or more | only what they share |
| Section | two | the intersection curve of their surfaces (not a solid) |
| Cross sections | one | a set of slice curves along a plane, to see how the shape changes |

Boolean inputs move under the result and are hidden; change an input (a primitive's size or position) and the result recomputes.

### Follow along {#l11-do}

<ol class="steps">
<li>New file, Part workbench. Cylinder: radius 13, height 8, position x = 3.</li>
<li>Another cylinder: radius 8, height 8, at the origin.</li>
<li>Select the large cylinder, Ctrl-select the small one, Cut. The volume should be {{l11.cut}} mm³.</li>
<li>Select the Cut, 3D Offset 1 mm.</li>
</ol>

### On your own {#l11-self}

1. Move the large cylinder to x = 0, y = 3: the eccentric points another way; the volume doesn't change.
2. With Common, intersect a cube and a sphere into a “rounded cube”.
3. Select the eccentric, Cross sections: XY plane, spacing 2, 4 sections, and look at the curves.
