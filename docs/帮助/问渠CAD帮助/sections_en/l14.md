### What you will be able to do {#l14-goal}

<ul>
<li>Tell faces, shells and solids apart: a shell is faces sewn together; only a closed shell can enclose a solid.</li>
<li>Sewing: Part Create from shape → Shell (or sew) joins faces into a shell; → Solid turns a closed shell into a solid.</li>
<li>Thicken: give an open surface a thickness with Part Offset → Thickness.</li>
<li>Thickness on a solid: pick the open faces and keep a wall inwards.</li>
<li>Check geometry: bad faces, gaps, self-intersections.</li>
</ul>

### Three routes {#l14-ways}

| What you have | How it becomes a solid |
|---|---|
| Several solids (extrusions, revolutions) | Booleans give a solid directly (lesson 13's Common) |
| Several surfaces that close up | select them, Create from shape → Shell, then → Solid; fix gaps first (refine, or sew with a tolerance) |
| One open surface (a thin-walled part) | Offset → Thickness, with thickness and direction |

The head in this module takes the first route: outlines extruded to solids, intersected and filleted — the result is already a closed solid.

### Head: hollowed into a casting {#l14-shell}

::video{src=wqcad-l14a-en title="Recording: turn to the bottom face, select it → Part Thickness −4 → a casting shell open at the bottom (about 50 s)"}

- A negative thickness (−4) grows inwards: the outside stays the same and becomes the casting wall.
- The selected face is opened; every other face keeps 4 mm.
- The result is {{l14.shell}} mm³. The head mesh in the machine is {{l14.mesh}} mm³ — it also has the narrower neck, internal ribs and bearing bosses, while this module made only the upper box's shell, so the two can't be compared directly.

### Check geometry {#l14-check}

Before machining, tooling or STEP export, select the solid and run Part Check geometry:

| What it checks | If there's a problem |
|---|---|
| Shape validity (BRep check) | face boundaries not closed, faces and edges don't match; go back to the feature and change its parameters |
| Self-intersection | most often fillets or thickness too large; reduce the radius or thickness |
| Volume and area (tick Run BOP check and Shape content) | check there is 1 solid and the shell is closed |

WenQuest CAD's Check geometry gives different results from official FreeCAD 1.1 — one of WenQuest CAD's kernel corrections (lesson 20) — so this step isn't recorded; go by what WenQuest CAD shows you.

### On your own {#l14-self}

1. Without Booleans: trim the two lesson-12 surfaces against each other (Part Split, or Surface workbench trim), select the remaining faces, Create from shape → Shell → Solid, and compare the volume with the Common.
2. Select one more face for Thickness (e.g. the right side) and see both opened.
3. Model the narrow lower part of the head (lesson 12, On your own 2) as a solid too, fuse it to the upper part and then hollow it.
