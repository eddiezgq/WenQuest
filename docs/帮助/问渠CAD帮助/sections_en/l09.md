### What you will be able to do {#l09-goal}

<ul>
<li>Polar pattern: n copies evenly around an axis; Linear pattern: n copies along a direction; Mirrored: one copy across a plane.</li>
<li>Multi-transform: several transformations stacked (e.g. linear, then polar).</li>
<li>Patterns copy <b>features</b> (pocket, pad, hole…), not sketches; change the original and all copies change.</li>
<li>Through-all pockets: when the sketch is on a datum plane outside the material, watch the direction (Reversed).</li>
</ul>

### Four transformation features {#l09-four}

| Feature | Parameters | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| PolarPattern | axis, angle (extent or spacing), occurrences | flange holes, spokes, teeth, knurling |
| LinearPattern | direction, length (extent or spacing), occurrences | rows of holes, fins, grooves |
| Mirrored | mirror plane | symmetric bosses and holes |
| MultiTransform | the above, stacked in order | hole grids, several rings of holes |

In Extent mode the length (angle) is from the first copy to the last; in Spacing mode it is between neighbours. A polar pattern over 360° wraps round, so 5 occurrences means one every 72°.

### Mirror and multi-transform {#l09-mirror}

- **Mirrored**: select a feature, click Mirrored, choose the horizontal/vertical sketch axis or a datum plane. The two ears of the WQ-LS needle clamp (lessons 3–4) could be one drawn and one mirrored.
- **MultiTransform**: select a feature, click MultiTransform, right-click Add linear pattern / polar pattern / mirrored in the panel and stack them. For example a ring of 6 holes, repeated 3 times along the axis.
- If a copy fails (outside the solid, not touching the original) it shows red and OK reports an error: reduce the spacing or the count.

### On your own {#l09-self}

1. Change to 6 holes of Ø16: edit only the pattern count and the original circle, and watch all six change.
2. Make the grip grooves in Spacing mode: 7.5° spacing, 48 occurrences; compare with Extent 360°.
3. With MultiTransform make a 3 × 4 hole grid at 10 pitch: linear X 4, then linear Y 3.
