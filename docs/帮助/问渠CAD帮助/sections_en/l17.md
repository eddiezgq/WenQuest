### What you will be able to do {#l17-goal}

<ul>
<li>A sheet = frame template + views + annotations. Views are projected from the 3D model; change the model and the drawing follows.</li>
<li>Choose the projection direction when inserting a view; lay long shafts horizontal (Rotate clockwise).</li>
<li>Dimensions: select edges in the view (Ctrl for more), click a dimension tool; position the dimension with its X and Y properties.</li>
<li>Detail views: centre, radius, scale; dimensions on a detail show true size.</li>
<li>Title block: click a field in the frame and type.</li>
</ul>

### The view and first dimensions {#l17-view}

::video{src=wqcad-l17a-en title="Recording: A4 frame → insert view, rotate clockwise to horizontal → move it → length 180, ⌀8 → detail view (oil grooves, radius 4, 5:1) (about 140 s)"}

- A view's position (X, Y) is measured from the sheet's lower-left corner, in millimetres.
- Diameter: TechDraw's Diameter dimension needs a circle; a shaft seen from the side is two lines, so measure 8 with a Vertical dimension and then Insert ⌀ prefix.
- The detail centre is relative to the main view's centre (the bar's centre is at 90 mm and the first groove at 39.25–39.75, so X ≈ 39.5 − 90 = −50.5; −49.5 takes in both grooves).

### Groove dimensions, title block {#l17-dims}

::video{src=wqcad-l17b-en title="Recording: 0.5, 1.5, 0.2 on the detail → 39.25 on the main view → title, material, drawing number, scale (about 120 s)"}

Compared with the machine's own LS-201 needle-bar drawing (generated from the 3D model in round 138): ⌀8, material GCr15 (AISI 52100) and scale 1:1 agree. LS-201 gives the length as 179 — measured over the cylindrical part between the 0.5 chamfers; this lesson measures between the end faces, 180. LS-201 doesn't dimension the two oil grooves (0.5 wide, 1.5 apart, 0.2 deep, 39.25 from the lower end); this lesson adds them. LS-201 also has ⌀8 h5, Ra 0.2 and technical notes — next lesson.

### Follow along {#l17-do}

<ol class="steps">
<li>Open the needle bar (or lesson 9's two-groove bar), TechDraw workbench, Insert page using template: A4 landscape.</li>
<li>Select the bar, Insert view, Rotate clockwise, OK. Properties X = 125, Y = 140.</li>
<li>Both end lines → Horizontal dimension 180; top and bottom outlines → Vertical dimension 8 → Insert ⌀ prefix.</li>
<li>Select the view → Detail view: X = −49.5, Y = 3.5, radius 4, custom scale 5. Properties X = 235, Y = 92.</li>
<li>On the detail: groove bottom → 0.5; land between grooves → 1.5; outer line + groove bottom → Vertical 0.2. Main view: left end + first groove's left side → 39.25.</li>
<li>Click the title-block fields: title, material, drawing number, scale.</li>
</ol>

### On your own {#l17-self}

1. Add a left view (tick Left in the projection group when inserting) and look at the chamfer circles at the end.
2. Make a Section view through a groove and look at its profile.
3. Change the detail to 10:1. Do the dimensions change? (No: they show true size.)
