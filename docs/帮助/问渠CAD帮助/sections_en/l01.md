### What you will be able to do {#l01-goal}

<ul>
<li>Say what the menu bar, toolbars, workbench selector, model tree, properties, 3D view, navigation cube, report view and status bar are for.</li>
<li>Use CAD navigation: middle-drag = pan, middle + left drag = rotate, wheel = zoom.</li>
<li>Switch between front / top / right / isometric views, and bring a lost model back with “Fit all”.</li>
<li>Select a part and read its data in the properties.</li>
</ul>

File used: the complete WQ-LS lockstitch machine (110 parts in eight groups).

### Mouse and views {#l01-nav}

WenQuest CAD uses the “CAD” navigation style by default (shown at the bottom right of the status bar):

| To… | Mouse | Keyboard / button |
|---|---|---|
| Pan | Hold the middle button and drag | — |
| Rotate | Hold the middle button, then the left button, and drag | Arrows on the navigation cube |
| Zoom | Wheel (around the mouse position) | — |
| Standard views | Click a face or corner of the navigation cube | 0 isometric, 1 front, 2 top, 3 right, 4 rear, 5 bottom, 6 left |
| Lost the model | — | “Fit all” (magnifier button) |
| Select a part | Left-click; click empty space to deselect | — |

::video{src=wqcad-l01b-en title="Recording: navigation, standard views, selecting (about 45 s)"}

### Preferences: units, language, navigation {#l01-pref}

Menu Edit → Preferences. The three settings people change most:

- **Units**: General → Language and number format → Unit system. For machine design use “Standard (mm, kg, s)”.
- **Language**: same group. Restart after changing it.
- **Navigation style**: Display → Navigation → 3D navigation. You may switch if you are used to another program; this course always uses “CAD”.

::video{src=wqcad-l01c-en title="Recording: where the preferences are (about 25 s)"}

### Follow along {#l01-do}

<ol class="steps">
<li>Start WenQuest CAD, click “Open File” on the start page and open the course file <code>WQ-LS.FCStd</code>.</li>
<li>Press 0 (isometric), then click “Fit all”.</li>
<li>Middle-drag the machine to the left; middle + left drag until you can see the handwheel; zoom in with the wheel until the needle bar is clear.</li>
<li>Press 1, 2, 3 to look from the front, top and right; click a corner of the navigation cube to go back to isometric.</li>
<li>In the model tree expand “Upper shaft &amp; take-up” and click <code>crank</code>: it turns green in the 3D view and its name and placement appear in the properties.</li>
<li>Click empty space to deselect.</li>
</ol>

### On your own {#l01-self}

1. Click the handwheel in the 3D view and see which tree item is selected.
2. Hide the “Housing” group (select it and press Space) to see the mechanism inside; press Space again to show it.
3. Open Preferences, check that the unit system is “Standard (mm, kg, s)”, then click Cancel.
