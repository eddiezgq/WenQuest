Manufacturing process for sewing-machine needles

Illustrative: 12 main operations; sequence and grouping vary between makers; values come from the plant's process sheets

1Incoming wireHigh-carbon steel wire, about shank diameter; check diameter, surface, decarburisation
2Straighten and cut offRoller straightening, cut into blanks; blank = needle length + allowance
3Blade reducingRotary swaging or centreless grinding thins the blade and forms the taper; shank chamfer
4Press long groove, short groove and scarfCold pressing in dies; groove depth and scarf position set the hook clearance
8TemperingReduces brittleness; balances hardness against toughness
7QuenchingHeated in protective atmosphere, oil-quenched; blade becomes hard
6Point grindingForm wheel grinds a sharp, ball or cutting point; avoid grinding burn
5Flatten and punch the eyeEye area pressed from both sides, then punched through; the burr-prone key step
9StraighteningHeat treatment distorts; each needle is straightened to spec
10Tumbling / electropolishingRemoves burrs; eye, groove and point become smooth
11Plating / coatingNickel or chrome plating, or wear-resistant coatings such as titanium nitride
12Inspection and packingAutomatic optical inspection of every needle; sampled hardness and straightness; boxing
