Retrofitting a mechanical lockstitch head: what comes off, what goes on, and where

Illustrative side view, not to scale. Circled numbers match the BOM rows of Table 20-1; dashed parts are removed

  • ① Servo motor + encoder (direct drive; handwheel on the motor's rear end)
  • ② Trimmer solenoid: under the bed beside the hook, swings the moving knife via a link
  • ③ Wiper solenoid: front of the arm, wiper sweeps under the needle
  • ④ Backtack solenoid: inside the arm, pulls the reverse lever of the stitch regulator
  • ⑤ Foot-lift solenoid: under the bed, pushes the knee-lift lever
  • ⑥ Tension-release solenoid: on the arm, pushes the release pin of the tension assembly
  • ⑦ Head-tilt switch (proximity switch)
  • ⑧ Pedal Hall sensor (under the table)
Removed: clutch motor, V-belt and guard, mechanical positioner (if any)
Or: motor under the table with a 1:1 timing belt (encoder index stays aligned with each spindle revolution)
