How the eye is made: steps and defects

Illustrative: cross-section through the eye centre, perpendicular to the blade axis; enlarged, not to scale

Common defects
  • Burrs and sharp edges: cut, fuzzed or broken thread
  • Eye off-centre: one wall too thin, needle breaks
  • Rough eye wall: friction heat, uneven stitches
  • Punch wear: burrs grow, eye width drifts
