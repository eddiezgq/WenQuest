About 90 million people work in the world’s textile and clothing industry; women make up about 80% of garment-manufacturing employment (International Labour Organization).
China produces about 6.85 million industrial sewing machines a year (2024, China Sewing Machinery Association data) and exports about 4.69 million; China makes roughly 70–80% of the world’s output.
