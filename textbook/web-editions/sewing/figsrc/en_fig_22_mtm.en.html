Bundle flow cannot do made-to-measureA bundle of 10–30 pieces must all be the same size to go through one operation together;
every made-to-measure suit differs in size and style, so it can only flow one piece at a time,
and every work centre has to know what “this particular piece” requires.
Costs and benefits of one-piece flowCosts: hangers, tags, terminals and data systems; operators must master several operations.
Benefits: little work in progress, short throughput time, every piece traceable.
A publicly reported suit factory: about 300 operations, delivery in about 7 working days.
