③ Target table written to the FPGA (the idle half of the double buffer)

target = (c + (i + ½)·p) / 0.01 mm, sorted in travel order; for the return course the MCU writes the mirrored table. The lead is not stored: the FPGA subtracts it at compare time from the live speed (Chapter 17 FPGA lab).

④ Selection bitmap per step (60 needles, up to 8 bits per step)

stepneedles servedS1 bitmap (LSB = first needle reached)

Successive actions of one step are 8 pitches apart: 67.7 ms at 0.6 m/s, far above the 3 ms pull-and-release cycle. In the reversal zone the MCU writes the next course into the other RAM half; the halves swap at reversal.
