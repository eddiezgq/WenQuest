σ: interchangeable 0.0196 mm; adjusted 0.0054 mm; after a needle change 0.0131 mm. Interchangeability reaches 99% only if every tolerance is tightened uniformly to 60%, raising cost by about two-thirds; adjustment adds only a few yuan per machine, and the absorbable links can then be relaxed.
