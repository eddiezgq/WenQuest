Make-up of the pattern-sewer prototype WQ-SC/PS

Illustrative. Left: mechanics (home-built X–Y table + an existing lockstitch head); centre: drives and actuators; right: control and networking. IDs marked * are proposed IDs, planned for the library

Mechanics (travel 300 × 200 mm)

X axis: clamp carriageBall screw 16×10 A-BSC-SFU; size-15 linear guide A-LGD-RAIL; motor via 1:1 timing belt A-PUL-HTD 3M-20; moving mass ≈ 4 kg
Y axis: beam (carries the whole X axis)Same sizes, two guide rails; moving mass ≈ 12 kg
Clamp and templateUpper and lower template plates, 2 double-acting cylinders D-PNU-CYL*, reed switches confirm clamping
Lockstitch head (existing)Feed dog removed; intermediate presser foot, trimmer and wiper added (as on WQ-SC/LS)

Drives and actuators

Spindle servo + encoderSame module as the lockstitch prototype D-MOT-PMSM* / D-DRV-SERVO*, 2500 lines with index
X, Y servos 400 W ×2D-MOT-PMSM* + D-DRV-SERVO*, CAN FD, position mode, 10 000 counts/rev → 1 μm
Solenoids ×3Trimmer, wiper, tension release D-SOL-PUSH*; driver D-DRV-SOL* (boost + chopped hold + Zener clamp)
Solenoid valves ×3Clamp left, clamp right, presser-foot lift D-PNU-VLV*
SensorsX, Y home and limits D-SNS-PROX*; thread-break photocell D-SNS-PE*; air-pressure switch; E-stop

Control and networking

WQ-SC main controllerD-CTL-WQSC*: spindle-encoder input and angle interrupts; 1 ms X–Y interpolation; pattern parsing and look-ahead
CAN FD I/O expanderD-IO-EXP*: valves, sensors, buttons
GatewayD-GW-WQSC*: web parameters, pattern download; MQTT to wq/sewing/…
Pedal / start button, touch panel (optional)D-SNS-HALL* / D-HMI-PANEL*
Power48 V / 24 V switch-mode supplies D-PSU-SMPS*; 220 V AC for the servos
CAN FD bus: controller ⇄ spindle drive ⇄ X drive ⇄ Y drive ⇄ I/O expander; the spindle encoder's differential A/B/Z goes straight to the controller (the angle reference never travels over the bus)
