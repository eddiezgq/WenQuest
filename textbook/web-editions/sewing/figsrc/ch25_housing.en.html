Key bores (illustrative)
  • a coaxiality ⌀0.01 mm; parallel to A 0.02/300
  • b coaxiality ⌀0.01 mm; centre distance to a ±0.02
  • c coaxiality ⌀0.008; perpendicular to a 0.01
  • c axis to b axis (hook) position ⌀0.03
  • Datums: A bottom, B rear face (hidden), C right end
Casting design points
  • ① keep walls even (illustrative 6–8 mm); core out thick spots
  • ② ribs stiffen arm and column (Section 23.6)
  • ③ fillet every corner against shrinkage and cracks
  • ④ core-removal holes, plugged after machining
  • ⑤ draft 1°–3° (sand), less for die casting
  • ⑥ oil passages and sump: cast or drilled, must be cleanable
