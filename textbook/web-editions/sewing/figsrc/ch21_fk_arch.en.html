Make-up of the scaled flat-knitting prototype WQ-SC/FK (single system, single bed, 60 needles, E5)

Illustrative. Top: mechanical layout (plan view, not to scale); bottom: electrical modules. Dashed outlines are home-made parts; solid outlines are existing library entries or modules proposed for the library (* proposed ID)

WQ-SC controller (frame)D-CTL-WQSC*: works per course: pattern → selection bitmaps, carriage position commands
Carriage board (rides along)MCU or MCU + small FPGA: fires per needle on carriage position; 8 selector, 2 carrier, 2 stitch-cam channels; CAN FD through the cable chain
Carriage drive200 W servo D-MOT-PMSM* + drive D-DRV-SERVO*; motor encoder 10 000 counts/rev → 0.01 mm
8-step selector (home-made)8 small electromagnets, Tr ≈ 1.5 ms (illustrative); driver D-DRV-SOL* with the same boost circuit
Stitch cams2 × closed-loop NEMA 17 steppers D-MOT-STEP* + lead screw C-SCN-LEAD
Yarn carriers2 push-pull solenoids D-SOL-PUSH*, picked up and dropped in the reversal zone
Take-down and tensionStepper D-MOT-STEP*; tension dancer + Hall angle sensor D-SNS-HALL*
ProtectionYarn break D-SNS-PE*, needle probe, home/limits D-SNS-PROX*, E-stop, cable chain
