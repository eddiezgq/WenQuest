Four drive trains (click to highlight)

NeedleMain shaft → needle-bar crank → needle-bar link → needle bar (slider-crank)
Take-upMain shaft → take-up crank pin → take-up lever (four-bar)
HookMain shaft → timing belt 1:1 → hook shaft → gears 1:2 → rotary hook
FeedMain-shaft feed eccentric → stitch regulator → feed rock shaft (horizontal); hook-shaft lift eccentric → lift rock shaft (vertical)

The stitch regulator and feed-lift mechanism are simplified from common link-type lockstitch machines; designs differ between manufacturers.
