Model: the needle bar moves as the Chapter 3 slider-crank (stroke 31 mm); the looper's left–right and front–back motions are both approximated as simple harmonic, so its point traces a flat ellipse. Looper stroke 30 mm, needle avoidance ±1 mm and the allowable rise at loop catching (borrowed from the Chapter 5 loop model) are illustrative values; retraction 3.2 mm, point 1.5 mm above the top of the eye and point-to-needle clearance 0–0.05 mm are taken from a manufacturer's manual. This is a teaching model, not design data for any particular model.
