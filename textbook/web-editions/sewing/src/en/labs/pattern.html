Tasks

  1. Pocket flap, fixed 2000 r/min: change the stitch length from 3 mm to 6 mm and count the stitches that cannot be completed; then, to what speed must you lower it before they all turn blue? Compare with Fig. 15-4.
  2. Change the fabric thickness from 1.5 mm to 6 mm. How many degrees is the window now? Why must thick material be sewn more slowly?
  3. Choose “Five-pointed star”, stitch length 6 mm, and raise the speed step by step from 1200 r/min: which stitches turn red first — those close to horizontal or vertical, or the diagonal ones? Explain with point 3 of Section 15.3.
  4. Switch to “Auto look-ahead” and compare the sewing times of the three motion laws; then set the margin to 15° — how much longer does it take? Write down which law you would choose for thin material and for thick material.
