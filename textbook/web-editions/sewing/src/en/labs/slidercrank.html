Lab tasks

  1. Change only the link length l (r fixed): does the stroke change? Which of the TDC and BDC accelerations grows and which shrinks?
  2. Change l from 55 mm to 40 mm. By how many degrees does the loop-catch moment (angle at 2 mm rise) move, and is it earlier or later? What does that mean for how the rotary hook is set?
  3. Raise the speed from 3000 to 6000 r/min. By what factor does the maximum inertia force increase? Explain with the formula.
  4. To keep the maximum inertia force within 400 N at n = 5000 r/min, what is the largest allowable reciprocating mass in grams?
