Book convention: 0° = needle-bar top dead centre. Gauge resolution 0.01 mm. Within about ±1.8° of TDC the gauge shows no change, so use the symmetric two-point method: find the same gauge reading on the way down and on the way up; the mean of the two encoder readings is bottom dead centre (should read 180°).

③ Trial sewing: N stitches then trim (slow motion), then statistics over 100 runs

Model (illustrative, same as the text): the spindle servo is the Chapter 13 model (J = 6×10⁻⁴ kg·m², Kp = 200 s⁻¹, Kv = 0.2 N·m·s/rad, Ti = 12 ms, peak 2.5 N·m, friction 0.15 N·m). The trim stitch runs at constant trim speed, the position loop engages at 25° (positioning speed 200 r/min) and the needle-up target is 70°; "stopped" = error ≤ 0.5° and speed < 10 r/min for 5 ms. Trim and release solenoids use the Chapter 13 model (R = 8 Ω, L 20→80 mH, 24 V boost 10 ms, 1 A hold, 48 V Zener clamp). Windows from Chapter 14: knife enters at 300°–330°, cut at 45°–65° of the next revolution (55° ± 10°, illustrative), tension released over 335°–55°. Wiper pull-in 12 ms, return 15 ms; foot lift 40 ms (illustrative). In the 100 runs, supply 24 V ± 10%, coil 8–9.5 Ω (cold/hot), speed ±2%, friction 0.12–0.18 N·m and load ±0.05 N·m are random.
