Hook-point clearance over 4000 machines (green band: permitted range 0.04–0.10 mm)

Contribution of each link to clearance variation (share of variance; grey = absorbed by assembly adjustment)

Model (illustrative, same as the book’s model20.py): nominal clearance 0.07 mm; each link’s error is normally distributed with tolerance = ±3σ; without adjustment, clearance = 0.07 + sum of link errors. If the clearance is set to 0.07 mm at assembly against the needle actually fitted (adjustment error ±0.01 mm), the housing bore positions, needle-clamp bore, hook-point dimension and the two needle links are all absorbed, leaving only needle-bar play and hook-shaft radial play; when the needle is changed, the two needle links take the new needle’s values. Machining cost = Σ coefficient × 0.03 / tolerance × 2 (halve the tolerance, double the cost); assembly adjustment costs 3 yuan per machine.

Model (illustrative, same as the book’s model20.py): the arm is treated as a single-degree-of-freedom system with modal stiffness 1×10⁸ N/m × (natural frequency/190 Hz)²; the exciting forces are 1st order (residual after balancing, 300 N at 5000 r/min) and 2nd order (120 N), both proportional to speed squared and to the reciprocating mass; response X = F/k × 1/√((1−r²)² + (2ζr)²), r = excitation frequency/natural frequency; RMS vibration velocity = 2πfX/√2; noise is estimated from 83.5 dB(A) at 5000 r/min (manual data, Chapter 8), adding 6 dB per doubling of vibration velocity. Criteria: vibration ≤ 8 mm/s over the working speed range (up to the current speed), noise at the current speed ≤ 86 dB(A) (illustrative).
