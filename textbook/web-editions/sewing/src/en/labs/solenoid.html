Sewing Machine Design and Manufacturing, Chapter 13 · Virtual lab 13-2 · How fast are the solenoids for thread trimming, reverse stitching and presser-foot lift? How many degrees of main-shaft rotation does the delay amount to?

Coil current (A), magnetic and spring force (N), armature stroke (mm); horizontal axis: time; vertical lines: switch-on and switch-off

Converted to main-shaft angle (the shaft rotation one trimming action takes at the current speed)

Model: coil resistance 8 Ω; inductance varies with the air gap, 80 mH with the armature pulled in and 20 mH fully open (equivalent magnetic-circuit gap 1 mm, stroke 3 mm); magnetic force F = ½ i² dL/dx; armature plus attached linkage referred mass 20 g; return-spring force = preload + 1 N/mm × stroke. Drive: full voltage first (boost), then after the set time a chopped hold current; at switch-off the current decays through the freewheeling path, whose voltage is 0.7 V for a diode or the Zener voltage for a Zener diode. Transistor voltage rating taken as 100 V. Parameters are illustrative values, the same as in the book’s model13.py.
