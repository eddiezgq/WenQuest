Sewing Machine Design and Manufacturing, Chapter 24 · Virtual lab 24-1 · A tiny clearance from economical accuracy; a control chart on the eye-punching operation

Diametral clearance of a batch of 2000 sets (green band: allowed 10–30 μm; red: out of tolerance)

Parts per group: blue hook bodies (race), orange baskets (rib); the difference is the leftover in that group

Model (illustrative; identical to the book’s figsrc/ch24_model.py, so the same seed gives the same numbers): race-diameter deviation x and basket-rib diameter deviation y are normally distributed; diametral clearance c = 20 + x − y (μm), allowed 10–30 μm. Every part is measured (measurement error σm); parts measured outside the drawing tolerance ±15 μm are scrapped; the rest are sorted by measured value into k equal groups over −15…+15 μm and paired only within a group; the surplus is left over. Cost: machining 10 yuan per part × (5 μm / σ) (halving σ doubles cost), measurement 0.5 yuan per part, group management 0.4 yuan per set × (k − 1); total cost divided by the number of good sets gives the cost per set.
