Tasks

  1. Select “None” and raise the carriage speed step by step from 0.4 m/s: at what speed do mis-selections start? Explain with t_n = p/v and v·T_d.
  2. Select “Fixed” lead and reduce the reversal margin from 200 mm to 10 mm: on which side of the fabric, and on which needles, do mis-selections appear? Why?
  3. Switch to “Real-time speed” and vary the gauge from E7 to E16: how fast can the carriage run without errors?
  4. Two-colour jacquard: compare the time per course and the estimated panel time for reversal margins of 40 mm and 150 mm, and state what margin is appropriate.
