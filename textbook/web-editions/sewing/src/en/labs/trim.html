Model (illustrative): needle-bar motion and the timing chart are taken from the worked examples of Chapters 3 and 8; the moving knife is driven directly by the solenoid through a linkage; knife entry angle = command angle + 6 × speed × delay, and the knife must enter the loop between 300° and 330° (the patent energises the solenoid when the needle point is about 10 mm above the throat plate, about 291° in this model); the moving knife cuts against the fixed knife at the take-up lever’s top position (55° of the next revolution); after the cut the servo stops at maximum deceleration (about 4400 rad/s², Chapter 13) and must stop 5° before the needle point enters the fabric (101.7°); the tension assembly must be released at least from 335° to 55° of the next revolution, otherwise the needle-thread tail is too short. The solenoid delay varies between the set minimum and maximum.
