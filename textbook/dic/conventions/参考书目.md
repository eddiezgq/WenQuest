# 《数字集成电路设计》参考书目（v0.1）

1. J. M. Rabaey, A. Chandrakasan, B. Nikolić. *Digital Integrated Circuits: A Design Perspective*. 2nd ed. Prentice Hall, 2003.（中译本：《数字集成电路——电路、系统与设计》，电子工业出版社）
2. N. H. E. Weste, D. M. Harris. *CMOS VLSI Design: A Circuits and Systems Perspective*. 4th ed. Addison-Wesley, 2011.
3. I. Sutherland, B. Sproull, D. Harris. *Logical Effort: Designing Fast CMOS Circuits*. Morgan Kaufmann, 1999.
4. S. M. Kang, Y. Leblebici, C. Kim. *CMOS Digital Integrated Circuits: Analysis and Design*. 4th ed. McGraw-Hill, 2014.
5. B. Razavi. *Design of Analog CMOS Integrated Circuits*. 2nd ed. McGraw-Hill, 2017.（MOSFET 模型）
6. SkyWater Technology / Google. *SKY130 Process Design Kit* documentation, skywater-pdk.readthedocs.io.（工艺与器件参数）
7. 本书的器件模型与校准记录：仓库 `textbook/dic/conventions/mos.py`、`samples/数字集成电路设计/生成脚本/calib/`.
