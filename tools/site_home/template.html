II

一节课怎么上How a lesson works

理工科每一节课都走同一条线。实际问题以机器人为主，再配一个生活里的例子。Every science and engineering lesson follows the same line. The problem is usually a robot, paired with one everyday example.

  1. 实际问题A real problem比如：机械臂抓传送带上的零件，要提前多少出手？Say: how early must an arm reach for a part on a conveyor?
  2. 概念The concept教材里讲清楚，每个数都由程序算出，不是手抄的。Explained in our textbook; every number is computed by code, not copied.
  3. 动画Animation3Blue1Brown 风格，表现静态图表现不了的变化。3Blue1Brown style, showing change a still figure cannot.
  4. 虚拟实验Virtual lab浏览器里调参数、看结果，任务完成自动打勾。试一个 →Tune and test in the browser; tasks tick themselves. Try one →
  5. 建模求解Model and solve写出模型、求解、检验，再说清模型在哪里失效。Build the model, solve, check, then say where it breaks.

V

机器人虚拟实验室Virtual robotics labs

物理仿真直接在浏览器里运行，调参、失败、重来只要几秒。AI 助教读你的实验曲线，指出现象和原因，但不代你完成。Physics runs right in your browser, so tuning, failing and retrying take seconds. The AI tutor reads your traces and explains what happened, without doing the work for you.

倒立摆方程已与 MuJoCo 3.4 逐步对照。实体机器人实验室在筹建中：先在仿真里做对，以后再上真机。The cart-pole equations are checked step by step against MuJoCo 3.4. A physical robot lab is being planned: get it right in simulation first, then on real hardware.
